LEGAL · PRIVACY

Your information should be handled with care.

This draft privacy notice explains the intended behaviour of this static website. It must be reviewed and updated before live lead collection or analytics are enabled.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This website is currently a static information and enquiry-preparation experience. The project planner and website-audit form prepare downloadable text files in your browser; they do not transmit form data to Udta Teer or a server in this implementation.

Information you choose to enter

Information typed into the planner or audit form is used in the browser to create the file you request. The site code does not submit those fields to a backend endpoint.

Browser preferences

The website may store your selected appearance (light or dark) and language preference in local browser storage so those choices remain available on your next visit.

Third-party services

Google Fonts is requested to provide typography. Your browser may connect to Google services to load the fonts. Hosting is provided through the website deployment platform selected by the site owner.

Contact and future changes

No public contact email or phone number is listed in this draft because verified details were not supplied for this build. Before adding lead capture, analytics, advertising pixels, chat tools or other integrations, this notice should be updated to explain the data collected, purpose, retention, processors and user rights.

Questions

For now, this page is a draft notice and does not provide an operational privacy contact. The site owner should add a verified contact channel before launch.
